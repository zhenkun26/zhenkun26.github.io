## Hi, I'm Yuzheng

Welcome to **yuran blog**, my personal space for learning, technology, and everyday life. I use this blog to keep a record of what I learn, what I build, and the small things I want to remember.

### What you'll find here

- **Technology and projects:** practical notes, experiments, and reflections on building things.
- **Learning and reading:** ideas from my studies and books, with room to revisit them as my understanding grows.
- **Everyday life:** personal essays, brief updates, and moments worth keeping.

Some entries will be full articles; others may be a short note or something I learned that day. This is a growing notebook, and the writing can grow with it.

### Behind the name

The name *Yuran* carries a sense of joy and ease. The blog's motto expresses the spirit I want to bring to this space:

> Where joy prevails, the record endures; where peace abides, the way unfolds.

For me, it is a reminder to enjoy the process of learning, put experiences into words, and move forward with a calm mind.

### Languages

This blog has Chinese and English sections. Use **中文 | EN** in the navigation to switch between them. English versions of individual posts will be added over time, so some articles may still be available only in Chinese.
